# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be straightforward, be accurate, and assume the other person is trying to make the project
better.

## Expected

- **Say what is true about the code.** KAURAX documents that it has no fault proof over its
  own execution, that a multisig decides disputes, and that nothing has been audited. That
  standard applies to conversation too: do not overstate what a change does, and do not
  understate what it breaks.
- **Criticise the work, not the person.** "This assertion passes vacuously because the step
  it targets consumes no proofs" is useful. "This is sloppy" is not.
- **Accept correction.** Several defects in this repository were found by someone running a
  documented command and reporting that it did not work.
- **Take security reports seriously and privately.** See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discrimination on any basis
- Publishing someone's private information
- Deliberately misrepresenting what the code does, in either direction
- Reporting a vulnerability publicly before maintainers have had a chance to respond

## Scope

Issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and any space where someone is
representing the project.

## Reporting

Contact the maintainers through the address in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports are
handled privately.

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions that violate this document, and may
temporarily or permanently ban a contributor for behaviour they judge harmful. Maintainers
who do not follow it themselves should be reported the same way.
